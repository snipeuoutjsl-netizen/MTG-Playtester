Card outline key

Green: you can play or cast this right now. On a permanent on the battlefield it means an ability you can activate now. It pulses while you can respond to something on the stack.
Red: the timing is fine but you cannot pay for it: not enough mana, or not the right colours. Hover the card to see which.
Gold: wrong timing. Wrong phase, the stack is busy, or you already played a land. Hover the card to see why. Gold pulsing cards in the opening hand or a discard are the ones you can pick.
⚡
Lightning badge: instant speed or flash. It can be cast whenever you have priority. The badge takes the colour of the outline.
