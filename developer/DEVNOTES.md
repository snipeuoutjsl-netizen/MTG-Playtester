## Lessons: things we broke once, do not repeat

- **Never position a core UI zone with `position: fixed` + `100vw` maths + `translate(-50%)` (v0.99.25-27 mulligan hand).** It worked in the headless test window and put the hand off the left edge of a real window (different size, DPI scale, side-column width, transformed ancestors make `fixed` relative to that ancestor). Keep the hand in the normal layout flow; only change sizes, z-index and add a dim layer (`body.mullpick` / `body.mullhand`, v0.99.28).
- **Any layout change to the hand, the mulligan or a modal must be checked at several window sizes** (the `mulligan hand stays on screen` test in `cr-rules.js` runs 1280, 1673 and 2509 wide, with and without images mode) and the check is that every hand card and the prompt box are fully inside the window. A screenshot at one size is not enough.
- **Pop-ups all open at the true centre of the window** (v0.99.28). Only `dock` (stack) and `resolve` windows have their own places; the mulligan steps move pop-ups above the hand. Do not add per-modal offsets or padding.
- The sidebar width is not constant (it grows with the window), so never hard-code `340px` or similar to find the table centre.

Effects that use number words above five ("Draw seven cards", "create six tokens", "put ten counters"...) are now recognised up to ten; before, Griselbrand's "Pay 7 life: Draw seven cards" paid the life but the draw was not offered.

**v0.41.0** Surveil and scry done properly. Surveil lands (Undercity Sewers, Meticulous Archive and the rest) enter tapped, then the surveil window opens for the "When this enters, surveil N" trigger and names the card that caused it. The window shows the cards you looked at in two groups: those that stay on top (with up/down arrows so you choose the order, first = next draw) and those leaving (to the graveyard for surveil, to the bottom for scry). One click moves a card between groups. If your library has fewer cards than N you look at all of them; with an empty library nothing opens. The window can no longer be cancelled by accident, and the log names every card put into the graveyard.

**v0.40.1** New top bar. The top bar is regrouped: logo and deck picker on the left, New game + On the play together, the six game actions (Undo, Shuffle, Draw, Untap all, Token, Stack) as one icon-and-label button strip, then status chips (green dot = ready, amber = needs attention), the version, and Tools & Settings on the right. On narrower windows the button labels hide and only icons show (hover for the name and shortcut). The version badge shows just the number; hover or click it for what changed.

**v0.40.0** Board frames + tidier pre-game screen. New Settings → Board frame: Fire, Ice, Grass, Storm, Shadow and Royal gold put a glowing, slowly moving coloured outline around the playmat and tint the borders of the zones, hand and top bar. Pick Custom to choose your own two colours. Animated glow can be switched off (still outline) and the glow strength has its own slider. Frames are independent of the playmat colour/image. The Pre-game actions screen is laid out properly: each opening-hand card shows its name, what its ability does, and its button; the hint and Continue button have room to breathe.

**v0.39.3** Cast-time vs trigger "choose one". The app now tells apart where a "choose one" sits. On an instant or sorcery it is chosen as you cast the spell. After "When/Whenever/At the beginning of" (Territorial Kavu: "Whenever this creature attacks, choose one") it is a triggered ability and is chosen when the trigger goes on the stack — casting the creature no longer opens the mode window. A "choose one" after a cost and colon is chosen when you activate the ability. The card reader says which kind each modal line is.

**v0.39.2** Auto-pay sees every colour a land can make. Auto-payment, the hand outlines (Playable / Can't pay) and the mana window now use the same land-colour rules as tapping a land by hand: printed mana plus every basic land type the board gives it. With Leyline of the Guildpact in play every land can pay for any colour; Blood Moon-style effects reduce nonbasic lands to red.

**v0.39.1** P/T box text always fits. The number inside the live P/T box now scales down with its length (3/4, 10/11, 12/13...) so it always stays inside the box, on the card and in the hover preview. Applies to every creature with changed or variable power/toughness.

**v0.39.0** Live power/toughness shown on the card. When a creature's power/toughness differs from what is printed (Cosmogoyf, Territorial Kavu, counters, anthems, until-end-of-turn pumps), a P/T box now appears over the printed box on the card, even when card pictures are on. Ring colour: green = bigger than printed, red = smaller, gold = changes with the game (a ★ value). The box flashes briefly whenever the number changes. The large hover preview shows the same box over the picture; hover the box for the reasons (printed value and every effect).

**v0.38.0** Choose-one / choose-two (modal) cards. The app now recognises "Choose one —", "Choose two —", "Choose one or both —", "Choose one or more —", "Choose up to N —" and Spree. Casting such a card (Kolaghan's Command, Cryptic Command, charms...) opens a window listing every mode; you pick the right number before paying, and the window will not let you pick too many or too few. Only the modes you chose resolve, in printed order. They show on the stack, and the effects panel offers just those (for example Kolaghan's Command: deal 2 damage). Modes the app cannot apply by itself are labelled "you apply this one by hand". Works for spells, triggered abilities (the mode is chosen as the trigger goes on the stack, e.g. Territorial Kavu) and activated abilities. Spree extras add their cost to the spell and the window shows the total. The card reader (How the app reads cards) shows each mode and whether the app applies it.

**v0.37.0** Cosmogoyf fixed, live power/toughness for any count. Cosmogoyf now works: its real text counts the cards you own in exile (power = that number, toughness = that number + 1). The app did not recognise that wording before, which is why it showed 0/0 or a question mark. Power/toughness that depends on a count is now read generically: cards in exile, creature/land/etc. cards in your graveyard, cards in hand/graveyard/library, your life total, and anything "you control" (Elves, other creatures, artifacts, legendary permanents...), with optional "plus N". New: "until end of turn" power/toughness changes. Right-click a creature for +1/+1, +2/+2 or −1/−1 until end of turn; effects that say "this creature gets +X/+X until end of turn" (prowess) are offered when they resolve. They wear off at cleanup and show in the hover note. Modal abilities such as Territorial Kavu's attack trigger now list each mode separately.

**v0.36.0** One effect list, more abilities and triggers. The card reader and the game now share one list of effects, so what "How the app reads cards" reports is exactly what the game does. New effects: opponent damage and life loss (Ballista pings, Bolt-style spells), +N counters, create tokens (e.g. two 1/1 Soldiers), Add mana (rituals like Dark Ritual), mill. Cards with several activated abilities (Walking Ballista) now offer each one. Costs are paid for you: mana through the payment window, {T}, sacrificing itself, removing counters, paying life. Costs the app cannot pay are listed for you to pay by hand. Instants and sorceries now open the suggested-effects panel whenever the app recognises an effect. New trigger events: when this leaves the battlefield, whenever you gain life, whenever this (or a creature you control) deals combat damage to a player.

**v0.35.0** The app reads cards: card model and timing rules. New: Tools & Settings > "How the app reads cards" (and right-click any card > "How the app reads this card"). Every rules line is classified as keyword, mana, activated, triggered, static, loyalty or spell effect and marked Understood, Partly or Not understood, with a coverage summary for your whole deck. Activation restrictions are now read from the card text and checked: as a sorcery, during your upkeep / draw step / end step / combat / before blockers, once each turn, if you control a (type), if you have no cards in hand, N or more cards in your graveyard. They apply to mana abilities and other activated abilities, and the green outline respects them. Static abilities are listed per line using the v0.34 engine.

**v0.34.0** Static abilities apply to everything. New static-ability engine: every permanent's rules text is read and each effect is applied to ALL permanents it describes, not just the cards tested so far. Covers "is all colors / is red / is colorless", "Each creature you control has vigilance if it's white...", and "Other Dragons you control get +1/+1" style power/toughness boosts, for subjects like each/other/all, colours, nonland, legendary, creature types (changeling counts) and more. Leyline of the Guildpact now makes every nonland permanent all five colours everywhere: the card colour, Scion of Draco's grants, colour conditions and the preview note all agree. Hand key simplified to Playable / Can't pay (not enough mana or wrong colours) / Wrong timing, with the exact reason on hover.

**v0.33.0** Hand reordering and a clearer key. Drag cards sideways in your hand to reorder them; a gold line shows where the card will land.
