# MTG Playtester: developer notes (history of every session)

A solo "goldfish" playtester for Magic: The Gathering decks. Runs entirely in your
browser, offline, on Windows (Edge, Chrome or Firefox). Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Road to 1.0 (version numbers now move slowly)

Version 1.0 means the rules engine follows the Comprehensive Rules closely enough that a normal goldfish game needs no hand corrections.
Until then each release is a small, single-purpose patch (0.90.1, 0.90.2, ...); the middle number only changes when a whole area is finished.
Planned areas, roughly in order (this list is a plan, not a promise of what is done):

1. Stack and priority: responses at every step, copying spells, storm/replicate/splice, split second, "can't be countered".
2. A general replacement-effect framework (rule 614): today Necrodominance, Hullbreacher, Time Vault and Mox Diamond are each handled on their own.
3. Linked "if you do" / "then" chains for any card (done so far for draw and discard only).
4. Costs and casting order (rule 601.2): cost increases and reductions in the right order, X, alternative plus additional costs together.
5. Targeting: protection, hexproof, ward, "can't be the target".
6. Combat: blockers, first strike, double strike, trample, deathtouch and damage assignment.
7. State-based actions (rule 704) and triggered abilities in the right order (rule 603.3b), including delayed and state triggers.
8. The layer system (rule 613) for power/toughness and granted abilities.
9. Turn structure: extra turns, skipped steps, additional combat phases and main phases.
10. An opposing board (creatures, ward, taxes) so effects like Vren's apply.
11. A rules test suite organised by Comprehensive Rules section, run before every release.

## Floating mana

- Double-click or press **T** on an untapped land to tap it for mana. If it can produce multiple colours, choose the colour to float.
- Floating mana appears in the compact Mana HUD and is automatically used first when paying spell costs.
- Mana empties when you move to the next step/phase, matching normal mana-pool timing.
- Right-click an untapped land for **Tap without floating mana** when you only want to mark it tapped.

## Table layout and turn structure

- The table is laid out like a playmat: creatures across the top, your library / graveyard / exile on the right, non-creature permanents (artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers) bottom-left, lands bottom-right, hand along the bottom. Double-click the library to draw.
- A phase bar shows the current step (Untap, Upkeep, Draw, Main 1, combat steps, Main 2, End, Cleanup). **Space** = next phase, **N** = next turn. You can click a later phase to jump ahead.
- "Auto-run start of turn" does untap, upkeep and draw for you and drops you in Main 1. Turn it off to step through them yourself.
- The opening hand (keep / mulligan), putting cards on the bottom after a mulligan, and discarding to seven at cleanup are blocking pop-ups: you must answer them before the game continues.
- Land / creature sorting uses card data. With no bulk file loaded, the app learns each card from Scryfall the next time you are online (saved for offline use).

- Instants and sorceries can be cast onto a visible stack; the stack resolves last-in-first-out and cannot be closed until empty.
- While the stack is active, normal-speed spells are blocked and instant-speed spells remain playable.
- Land plays can stop for shock-land life/tapped choices and Scry/Surveil ETB prompts.
- Top 10 library inspection/reordering supports Brainstorm/Ponder-style learning exercises.
- Optional pause-at-phase-start mode stops the game at each step so triggers and priority can be discussed.
- Battlefield cards support separate +1/+1 and -1/-1 counters.
- Settings supports a custom card-back/sleeve image.
- The right-side Library remains above Graveyard and Exile, with a wider side panel so those zones are easier to see.

## Teaching mode settings (each aid is an independent switch since v0.22)

Teaching aids are now grouped under **Settings → Teaching mode**. The master switch and individual toggles let experienced players remove the instructional overlays without changing the underlying playtest controls.

Available aids include:
- Card playability indicators
- Phase explanations and the phase/step timeline
- Pause-at-step teaching stops
- Stack and priority guidance
- Mana-payment selection guidance
- Triggered-ability prompts
- Library hover previews
- Combat attack/block helper
- Rules hints

The playtester also includes a lightweight priority/stack flow, selectable mana payment for supported mana costs, activated-ability objects on the stack, basic triggered-ability detection, and combat declaration helpers. These are teaching-oriented approximations rather than a complete Magic rules engine.


## Rules-aware card timing and Domain (v0.07)
- Lands can be played only during either main phase, with an empty stack, and only if the normal land play is still available.
- Nonland spells use the stack. Instants and cards with Flash can be cast outside main phases; other spells require a main phase and an empty stack.
- Resolving permanent spells (creatures, artifacts, enchantments, planeswalkers and battles) puts them onto the battlefield; instants and sorceries go to the graveyard.
- Cards whose Oracle text contains Domain show a live `Domain X/5` badge. Domain counts distinct basic land types among lands you control, not the number of lands. Effects such as Leyline of the Guildpact, Dryad of the Ilysian Grove and Prismatic Omen make all five types count.

## Contextual mana costs and paying (v0.18)
- Cards that say "This spell costs {N} less to cast for each …" (Scion of Draco, Leyline Binding and other Domain cards, plus common "for each creature / artifact / land / card in your graveyard" wordings) show their real cost. The cost badge on the card turns gold and the preview says "Costs now …"; the payment window shows the printed cost and the reduction. Only generic mana is reduced, never below zero.
- Playability checks and casting now count floating mana. Floating mana is always spent first; lands are pre-selected only for what the pool cannot cover (none, if the pool pays the whole cost). With Teaching → Stack guidance off, spells are paid automatically the same way.

## Enters-tapped lands (v0.19)
Lands and permanents that say "enters the battlefield tapped" now enter tapped when played, cast, dragged or fetched (triomes, bounce lands, temples, gain lands). Conditional ones are checked too: fast lands ("unless you control two or fewer other lands"), slow lands ("two or more other lands") and check lands ("unless you control a Forest or a Plains"). Shocklands still ask whether to pay 2 life. Other conditions (such as reveal lands) are left untapped, so tap them by hand if needed. A pop-up and a log line say what happened.

## Keyword tooltips (v0.20)
Hover a card to see it in the preview panel. Keywords in its text (Flying, Deathtouch, Cascade, Suspend, Cycling, Ward, Domain and about 50 others, plus Leyline-style opening-hand abilities and "enters tapped") are underlined in gold, and a "Keywords" row of chips appears under the card, including in image mode. Hover any of them for a plain-language explanation of what it does.
